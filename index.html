02The challenge

Students neededmore personalizedfeedback.

One-to-one attention is what moves a writer forward, and a full timetable takes it away first. Students were stuck before they began, staring at a blank page.

03Research / Insight

We listenedto studentsand teachers.

The team started in the classroom, co-designing with the experts themselves. Two problems surfaced fast: grading essays by hand is laborious and inefficient, and students freeze at the blank page long before feedback is ever needed.

Classroom co-designStudent & teacher interviewsWorking prototypesBeta tests in schools
04The solution

An AI writing tooldesigned forreal classrooms.

Contextual prompts help students organise their thoughts and start writing — without doing the work for them. The assistant then drafts comments against the criteria each teacher sets.

Teacher in controlThe teacher picks which feedback is relevant for each student. Nothing reaches a student without teacher approval.

01Case study

Cultureas Catalyst

Moderna × IDEO

Moderna defines its Mindsets
to preserve its future.

Scope · Culture research · Mindsets · Onboarding revampPartnered · 2021 · Codified in weeks
People → Culture → A healthier tomorrow
02The challenge

Rapid growthput culture at risk.

In early 2020 Moderna took a COVID-19 vaccine into human trials 42 days after finalising the sequence — a pharmaceutical record engineered by a culture built on speed and accepted risk. Then it hired hundreds of people from established pharma, where bureaucracy is higher and risk tolerance lower. Here, delay is not a missed launch date; it is measured in lives.
